PestPredict SP - Predictive Pest Intelligence

About the Platform

PestPredict SP is a geospatial predictive intelligence system engineered to map and forecast infestation risks for both urban vectors and agricultural/forestry pests across the State of São Paulo.

By cross-referencing real-time weather data (temperature, relative humidity, and accumulated precipitation) with environmental factors and pest phenology, the model calculates regional susceptibility for key species including Tityus serrulatus (yellow scorpion), Diatraea saccharalis (sugarcane borer), and major silviculture pests.
